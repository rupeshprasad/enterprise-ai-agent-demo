# Customer Ordering Policy

## Verified Customers

Customers with Approved verification status may place orders with no verification-related restriction.

## Pending Verification

Customers whose verification status is Pending cannot place orders until verification is completed. The customer can always submit a request for review.

## Rejected Verification

Customers whose verification status is Rejected cannot place orders. The customer can always submit a request for review.

## Review Requests

A verification review request may be created for customers whose verification status is Pending or Rejected. To do that either customer can call customer service at +91-123-456-789 or a customer-service representative can use the agent to create a ticket on the customer's behalf.

## Ordering Support

If a customer is unable to place an order, they can submit a review request to get support.

##Exception
If a customer is ordering less than $100 then even pending users can place an order
If a customer is from india then there is no restriction and verification is not necessary
