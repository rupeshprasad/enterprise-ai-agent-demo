# Customer Ordering Policy

## Verified Customers

Customers with Approved verification status may place orders with no verification-related restriction.

## Pending Verification

Customers whose verification status is Pending cannot place orders until verification is completed.

## Rejected Verification

Customers whose verification status is Rejected cannot place orders.

## Review Requests

A verification review request may be created for customers whose verification status is Pending or Rejected.

## submitting the review request
To submit review request customer can reach out to customer service at this number 123456789

## Exception for ordering
If a customer is ordering less than $100 then even pending users can place an order
